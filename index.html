Tourist Spots From Kanyakumari

Vattakottai Fort

Step into history at this 18th-century coastal fort offering stunning views of the sea and Western Ghats. Perfect for a peaceful walk, great photos, and a dose of heritage by the waves.

Mathor Aqueduct

Walk along one of South India's highest aqueducts, offering breathtaking views of lush green valleys and distant hills. A unique blend of nature and architecture, perfect for thrill-seekers and photo lovers!

Sunset View Point

Watch the sun dip into the ocean where three seas meet. A breathtaking moment of colors and calm – a must-see highlight of your Kanyakumari trip!
